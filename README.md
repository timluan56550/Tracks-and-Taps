# Track and Taps
A fun decoding and encoding morse code puzzle game!

### Goal
---
- Your Role: You are a railway telegraph operator at the central office.
- The Mission: Guide the train safely across kilometers over a 10 round shift! 
- The Problem: You cannot flip the switches at junctions, instead your coworker at a different tower relies entirely on your Morse signals to flip the tracks the safe way! 


### How to play (There are instructions in the demo)
---
1. Decode the Morse signal sent by your coworker
2. Using logic, pick the path that is the safest based on the Coworkers decoded message
3. Type the town name down that path back in Morse code.
4. Hit send and watch the train to see if your signal was clear or not!

### Some features
---
- You can type the morse code using these keys ".", "-", "Space", and "/". These represent a dot, dash, letter break, and word break respectfully.
- You may also use "m", "x", "z". Press m for a dot, press m longer for a dash. Press x for a letter break (space). Press z for a work break.
- Every round there is either a 2-way or 3-way junction
- The amount of time you get per round decreases slightly. If it runs out, your coworker makes a blind pick! (Chance to succeed or fail)
- Cool pixel art of train animations
- A narrator box to see what happened!
- There is a morse code cheat sheet on the right

| Key | Action |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| m (Tap/Hold) | Tap for Dot, Hold for Dash |
| . | Dot |
| - | Dash |
| Spacebar or x | Letter Break |
| / or z | Word Break |
| Backspace | Deletes the previous letter |
